> README // WHAT THIS IS NOT ▸

The idea

Not a randomness test of one stream — the version the Global Consciousness Project actually measured: coherence across a network of unrelated sources. The signal is never any single feed; it's whether they fall into agreement more than independence allows.

The nineteen arms

Grouped Physical (solar wind, seismic, barometric, river flow, tide, air quality, ocean swell, hazards) · Random (two crypto null-arms, drand, NIST) · Market (BTC tape) · Human (Wikipedia, Bluesky, Hacker News, GitHub, Reddit, Mastodon). Each is read each epoch and standardized against its own running baseline (EWMA) into a deviation zᵢ.

The metric

Per epoch Z = Σzᵢ/√N over active arms; coherence c = Z², which is χ²₁ (mean 1) under independence. Co-movement makes |Σzᵢ| large → c spikes. The walk is Σ(c−1); session significance Zc = Σ(c−1)/√(2N) ≈ N(0,1) under the null.

Why the null arms matter

The two crypto.getRandomValues streams are genuinely independent N(0,1). They anchor calibration — watch pure chance refuse to synchronize — and make false coherence harder, not easier.

What this is NOT / honest caveats

It measures coincidence, not meaning. Jung's synchronicity is the significance an observer projects onto a coincidence; no instrument supplies that. Two specific cautions with this many arms: (1) the six Human feeds share a weak common driver — a major news event can move them together, so coherence confined to that cluster is mundane, not cosmic; broad coherence across Human and Physical and Random is the interesting case. (2) GDACS partly overlaps the seismic arm (both spike on big quakes) — for maximum rigor, comment it out. Deliberately excluded to avoid counterfeit synchrony: a second crypto (≈BTC), K-index & GOES X-ray (≈solar wind), BTC mempool (≈price), EONET (≈GDACS), ISS/tide-table (deterministic). And run long enough and you will cross p<0.05 ~1 session in 20 by chance — calibration, not a message. The "glitch" is theatre for a spike; the verdict text stays honest.
